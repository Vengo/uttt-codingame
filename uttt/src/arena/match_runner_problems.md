## 1. Challenges
- **Color Parity**: Neutralizing first-player advantage in Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe by alternating which bot plays as Player X and Player O across an even number of games.
- **Aggregated Match Statistics**: Tracking total wins, losses, draws, average move durations, and forfeits per participant.
